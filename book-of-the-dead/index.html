Book of the Dead: The Papyrus of Ani

Working educational study · v2.5. Explore 37 scanned sheets from the 1894 printed facsimile, the historical English translation attributed to E. A. Wallis Budge (1895), and an interpretive spatial index. The images are facsimile scans, not new photographs of the original manuscript.

Reading the source. The historical quotation, editorial guide and map associations are different layers. A clickable column identifies an annotated region and its assigned passage; it is not a word-for-word decipherment of every hieroglyph. Boundaries and numbered highlights may be approximate. The corpus and its inherited map associations remain under review.

The real 3D map. The cylindrical architecture, coordinates and optional study route are modern presentation choices—not a recovered Egyptian floor plan or an obligatory ancient itinerary. Gold marks the selected passage context. Rotate, zoom or select a named place to explore the linked records. A non-3D fallback retains the passage links.

How to explore. Scroll sideways or use the minimap to move through the wall. Select a figure or column to inspect the assigned text. Search finds names, spell numbers and phrases; the reader’s copy-link control shares the selected passage or column. “Begin the journey” follows the editorial sequence of sheets.
